# P6: motive definition decision (PROPOSAL; manuscript body unchanged until 「反映してください」)
Date: 2026-10-15. Source of the decision: user proposal (adopt Gunter et al. 2021 definition; disclose definition-dependence).

## Rule
- Primary a/b analysis uses Gunter et al. (2021, Table 1): a = offsetting by another tax; b = inherited fiscal (deficit- or debt-driven); procyclical, countercyclical, long-run-growth, within-VAT and spending-offset changes are excluded.
- Episodes outside Gunter's sample (start 1983:Q4): classified by P6 applying the same rule, flagged "P6-coded". Reported with and without them.
- P6's original definition (procyclical counted as b; labels fixed before estimation, D7) goes to the appendix as a sensitivity row.
- Disclosure sentence (draft): "The a/b comparison depends on how motives are defined. We adopt the definition of Gunter et al. (2021), fixed by an external source; our original coding, which treated procyclical consolidation as b and was fixed before estimation, gives different results (Appendix Table A1). We switched after seeing that the two differ."

## Reading
- Sign is negative in every variant; significance depends on definition and sample. Core a-group has only 3 episodes (NZ2010, GB1979, GB1991).
- Claim strength: exploratory heterogeneity, not a headline result.
- Caveat: the rule was adopted after seeing the P6-vs-Gunter difference in the extended sample; disclose.
